Postdoctoral Researcher, [Keller Lab](https://keller-lab.github.io/) · Thayer School of Engineering, Dartmouth College

Welcome to my website! I completed my PhD in UC Berkeley's Department of Statistics in August 2026, advised by Jon McAuliffe and Fernando Pérez. I am broadly interested in applications of statistics to problems in environmental and earth science, particularly the use of extreme-value statistics techniques to understand the risk of extreme weather and climate events in a world of climate change. In particular, my postdoctoral work combines themes of uncertainty quantification, hazard model emulation, risk quantification, and decisionmaking.
